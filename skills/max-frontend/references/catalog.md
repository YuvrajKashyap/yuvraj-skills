# Ideas to play with

- In a lighting showroom, let someone aim the fixture and watch its beam reveal the texture of stone, linen, or glass. The interaction teaches something a product photo cannot.
- For a music release, let the listener pull apart a chord into individual voices. Give each voice its own shape and let them come together as the chord resolves.
- On a reading surface, try one generous illustration that changes meaning as the reader reaches a particular sentence. Let the surrounding pages breathe.
- Give a familiar action a precise physical response. A dial might resist gently as it reaches a detent, and its readout could settle at exactly the same instant.

Use anything here as a starting point, or invent a direction that suits the brief better.
