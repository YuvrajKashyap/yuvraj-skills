# MAX Frontend

MAX frontend means MAX ambition across animation, reactivity, cinematic direction, and premium finish. Aim to impress and surprise the user. Let a gesture change the scene in a way someone can feel. Give a reveal the timing of a great cut. Decide for yourself where that intensity will have the greatest impact. Take their brief seriously and give yourself creative freedom wherever they leave room. Find something in the subject itself worth bringing to life. If you could swap in another brand's name and the whole experience would still make sense, look closer at what makes this one interesting.

When 3D belongs, go big with it. Give the object presence. Think about its silhouette, the way light moves across its material, and what someone could discover by turning it in their hands. On a speaker maker's site, for instance, pulling a cabinet apart in 3D could let someone see how the sound travels. Follow a better idea whenever you have one. A beautifully paced essay or an instrument panel that responds instantly to a musician's touch can be just as ambitious.

Carry the idea through the actual experience. Give the typography, composition, imagery, and small interactions the same care as the most spectacular moment. Make it feel premium on a phone and a pleasure to use with a keyboard or reduced motion. Choose the tools and techniques that best serve this project.

Look at what you built through the eyes of someone seeing it for the first time. Where does it still feel like the obvious first draft? Go back to that part and take a real creative swing. Sometimes the improvement is a richer scene. Sometimes it's clearing enough space for one extraordinary detail to land.

If inspiration would help, browse the [idea collection](references/catalog.md) or the [implementation examples](references/recipes.md). Borrow, reinvent, go beyond them, or skip them entirely. Your own strongest idea belongs here.
