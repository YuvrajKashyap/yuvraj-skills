# Rca Sweep

Turn the finding into a search predicate based on behavior. If a cleanup callback can overwrite a newer selection, search for asynchronous completions writing to replaceable state. Include equivalent APIs, wrappers, and older implementations.

Classify each candidate by reachability, ownership, and available guard. Inspect at least one apparent match that is safe to understand what protects it. Confirm likely matches with controlled inputs and record the exact preconditions.

Deliver a short table of affected paths, verified safe paths, and unresolved candidates. Group repairs only where the same invariant and verification genuinely apply. A textual match alone is insufficient reason to edit code.
